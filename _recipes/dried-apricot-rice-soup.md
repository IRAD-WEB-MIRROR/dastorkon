Every Friday in Chicago I made this soup, because it was the only way I could keep the fast and still feel at home. I bought dried apricots from a Turkish shop near the lake, and they were never quite right, but the smell of them simmering with onion made the small apartment feel like Orto-Ayyl for an hour.

Now I buy them from the women at the bazaar who sell [dried fruit](https://visitirad.com/shopping/) by the scoop, and I let Aruu choose, which means the stickiest ones. It is a plain [fast-day](../../pantry/#savma) soup, with no meat, milk or egg in it, and nobody who eats it at my table asks where the meat is. Eat it hot on a grey day, my dear, with bread to dip.

## Method

1. Soak the apricots. Rinse them, put them in a bowl, cover with hot water and leave for 20 minutes, until plump.
2. Prepare everything. Drain the apricots, saving the soaking water, and cut them in halves. Rinse the rice in several changes of water until it runs clear, then chop the onion and dice the carrot.
3. Fry the vegetables. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the onion and carrot for 8 minutes, stirring often, until soft and lightly golden.
4. Add the water. Pour in the water and a few spoonfuls of the apricot soaking water, add the salt and pepper, and bring to the boil, about 7 minutes.
5. Cook the rice. Add the rice, lower the heat and simmer, partly covered, for 12 minutes, stirring now and then so it does not stick.
6. Add the apricots. Stir in the apricots and sugar and simmer for 13 minutes more, until the rice is very soft and the apricots have melted into the broth.
7. Season and taste. Add a squeeze of lemon, then taste for salt and sourness, adding a little more of either if it needs it.
8. Serve hot. Ladle into deep bowls and top with the walnuts and dill.
