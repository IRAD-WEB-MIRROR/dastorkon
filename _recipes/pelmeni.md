In Nurzhan, on the first cold Sunday of the year, Olga's mother Tamara set a long board across her kitchen table and the whole family sat down to fold pelmeni. I was a student then, home from Talas for a weekend, and she gave me the worst seat, the one nearest the door, and a pile of dough circles. My first dumplings looked like crumpled hats. She ate every one.

Pelmeni came to us in the Soviet years and stayed in every Russian-speaking freezer. In Irad we make them with beef and lamb, never pork, and I serve them with butter and a spoon of qatiq from [Talas Süt](https://talas-sut.com/products.html#fermented). Last Sunday Aruu and I folded ninety in front of the radio. She counts them aloud, my dear, and she is always short by three.

## Method

1. Make the dough. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl, stir in the egg and the cold water, then knead for 10 minutes into a firm, smooth dough.
2. Rest the dough. Wrap it in a damp cloth and leave it for 30 minutes at room temperature, so it rolls out without springing back.
3. Mix the filling. Work the beef, lamb, onion, garlic, salt and pepper together in a bowl, then knead in the cold water a spoonful at a time until the mince is soft and sticky.
4. Roll the dough. Cut it in 4 pieces and roll each one on a floured surface until it is 1.5 mm thin, keeping the others covered.
5. Cut the rounds. Press out circles about 5 cm wide with a small glass or cutter, and gather the scraps to roll again.
6. Fill and pinch. Put a level teaspoon of filling in the middle of each round, fold it over, pinch the edge shut, then bring the two corners together and press them to make a little ear.
7. Keep them apart. Lay the pelmeni on a floured tray, not touching, and cover them with a tea towel until all are folded; this takes about 40 minutes with a helper.
8. Boil the pelmeni. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil while you shape, drop in half of the pelmeni, stir once and simmer for 7 minutes after they float, until the filling is cooked through (75°C). Lift them out with a slotted spoon and boil the second batch the same way.
9. Serve hot. Toss the pelmeni with the butter until glossy, then top with dill and black pepper and bring them to the table with the qatiq.
